2. Wholesale Price Leakage

Wholesale Road Bikes lost -$1.17M and Touring Bikes lost -$1.24M due to dealer catalog prices set below standard manufacturing costs.

Root-Cause Analysis

Negative Margin Leakage in Wholesale Road & Touring Bikes

Empirical SQL audits confirmed that wholesale dealer catalog prices on finished Touring and Road bikes were established below standard manufacturing costs. When combined with promotional volume discounts (up to 20%), realized line margins plunged down to -247%, generating over -$2.4M in aggregate margin drainage.
